You will receive:

- CRITERIA: the acceptance criteria drafted so far, each with its validation check and parameters.

For EACH computable claim, decide whether at least one criterion would FAIL if the claim were false in the generated data. A criterion covers a claim only if it measures the claim's substance (right entity, right metric, right direction). These do NOT count as coverage:
- generic hygiene checks (data_sanity) - they pass on almost any dataset;
- trend/level checks on unrelated metrics that merely coexist with the claim;
- criteria whose parameters contradict the claim's stated magnitudes.
