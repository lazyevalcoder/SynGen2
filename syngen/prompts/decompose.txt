You are a senior Revenue Operations analyst. You will be given a business narrative. Your job is to reverse-engineer it into measurable acceptance criteria that a generated dataset must satisfy for the story to be true.

Rules:
- Every criterion MUST be computable from tabular data (accounts and opportunities tables). No subjective criteria.
- Give each criterion a target value and a tolerance.
- Flag any claim in the story that is ambiguous or under-specified, and state the assumption you chose to resolve it.
- Classify each criterion as either "parametric" (fixable by adjusting distribution parameters) or "statistical" (needs sample size or seed changes).

User decisions already made (override any default you would otherwise choose):
{{user_decisions}}

Story:
{{story}}

Context you may assume:
- Data model: accounts table + opportunities table (list price, realized price, discount pct, close date, stage, region, segment, owner). When the story references open pipeline / stages / commit categories / deals in progress, opportunities also carry expected_close_date, some rows remain OPEN with lifecycle stages (not Closed Won/Lost), and an opportunity_stage_history sheet records each open deal's current stage and entered_date. When the story references products/SKUs/tiers/margins/COGS, opportunities also carry product_id, product_tier (e.g., entry/core/premium) and cogs_ratio. When the story references territories or sales regions grouped into areas, both tables carry territory. When the story references plans/quotas, a quota_plan sheet (per-segment OR per-territory quarterly revenue targets) is generated too and revenue_vs_plan measures attainment against it. When the story references AE headcount, hiring, ramping reps, or capacity vs plan, a reps roster and capacity_plan sheet (per-territory/region quarterly headcount plan vs actual, ramping counts, derived effective_capacity_pct) are generated too and effective_capacity checks them. When the story references accounts changing hands or being unowned, an account_ownership sheet (per-account-per-quarter owner) is generated. When the story references meetings/touches/engagement, an account_activity sheet (touches per account per quarter) is generated. When the story quotes a forecast/commit number vs actual, a forecast_snapshot sheet plus in_commit flags on won deals are generated. Whale/outlier stories mark deals with is_outlier; quota.attainment_ex_outliers can then split headline vs core attainment.
- Product tiers carry configurable gross margins and optional per-quarter COGS inflation; discount levels can be coupled to tier margin (WS4 correlation).

Respond with ONLY a JSON object:
{

      "name": "short name matching the definition exactly",
      "check": "one of: win_rate_flat | avg_discount_quarter | discount_trend_monotonic | region_discount_premium | end_of_quarter_effect | realized_vs_list | data_sanity",

The optional "depends_on" array lists IDs of criteria whose targets are
mathematically linked to this criterion's target, such that changing one
requires re-reviewing the other. Example: realized_vs_list (realized/list
percentage) is the complement of avg_discount_quarter for the same quarter,
so it must depend on that criterion. Only add edges you are confident about;
empty array when none.

Available checks and their params:
- win_rate_flat: {band_pp}
- avg_discount_quarter: {quarter, target_pct, tolerance_pp}
- discount_trend_monotonic: {max_dip_pp}
- region_discount_premium: {region, vs: [regions], min_premium_pp, quarters: [labels]}
- end_of_quarter_effect: {window_days, min_gap_pp}
- realized_vs_list: {quarter_start, target_start_pct, quarter_end, target_end_pct, tolerance_pp}
- data_sanity: {max_discount_pct}
- cycle_length_trend: {min_increase_pct} - average won-deal cycle time (close minus created, days) must grow from first to last quarter by at least this percent. Use for "deals are taking longer to close".
- creation_volume_trend: {target_decline_pct, tolerance_pp} - opportunity creation change from first to last quarter lands within tolerance of the claimed decline (story says pipeline slowed ~20% -> target_decline_pct=20, tolerance_pp=8).
- deal_size_trend: {target_change_pct, tolerance_pp} - average won-deal size (list price) change from first to last quarter lands within tolerance of the claim. Story says deal sizes shrank ~15% -> target_change_pct=-15.
- icp_creation_shift: {min_increase_pp} - share of created pipeline from NON-ICP accounts must rise by at least this many percentage points from first to last quarter. Use for "pipeline growth came disproportionately from low-quality accounts".
- revenue_concentration: {top_n, min_top_share_pct} - top N deals' share of last-quarter won revenue must be at least this percent. Use for "revenue depended on a handful of large deals".
- revenue_vs_plan: {segment, target_pct, band_pct, dimension?} - attainment of quarterly revenue plan vs quota targets. Use when the story references quotas/plans or "missed/exceeded plan by X%". target_pct is the story's expected attainment: missed plan by ~5% -> target_pct=95; beat by ~4% -> target_pct=104. band_pct is tolerance (2 is a good default). segment may be a named segment/territory OR "_all_" for company-wide attainment claims (e.g., "revenue finished at 101% of plan"). For territory plans add "dimension": "territory". Do not invent other aggregate names like "Total" or "Overall" - use "_all_" for those claims.
- blended_margin_trend: {target_change_pct, tolerance_pp} - blended gross margin % of won deals changes from first to last quarter within tolerance of the claim. COGS rising -> margin falling: "gross margin eroded ~3 points" -> target_change_pct=-3, tolerance_pp=1.
- tier_share_shift: {tier, from_share_pct, to_share_pct, tolerance_pp} - one product tier's share of WON REVENUE moves from ~from_share_pct (first quarter) to ~to_share_pct (last quarter). Use for mix-shift claims ("entry-tier products grew to 40% of sales", "mix moved to premium").
- discount_margin_link: {high_margin_tier, low_margin_tier, min_gap_pp} - avg discount on the high-margin tier must exceed the low-margin tier's by at least min_gap_pp. Use for "discounts were concentrated in high-margin products".
- avg_price_by_tier: {tier, max_avg_realized_usd} - average realized price of won deals in a tier stays under the cap. Use for entry-price / price-point claims ("SMB bought only entry-level deals around $15k").
- gap_concentration: {dimension, min_bottom_gap_share_pct} - the bottom quartile of plan units (by attainment) must hold at least this share of total plan shortfall. Use for "the miss was concentrated in weak territories" (dimension: territory).
- stage_aging: {stale_threshold_days, max_stale_share_pct} - share of OPEN pipeline older than the threshold (measured at last quarter end) stays under this percent. Use for "pipeline is decaying/stale".
- slippage_trend: {min_increase_pp} - the share of open deals whose expected close has slipped past their creation quarter's end rises from first to last quarter by at least this many points. Use for "deals are slipping into next quarter".
- coverage_ratio: {quarter, min_multiple, target_quarter_offset?} - open-pipeline VALUE closing around `quarter` divided by that quarter's revenue plan target. Use for "coverage was 3.5x" style claims (requires a quota block; set target_quarter_offset=-1 to measure against the PRIOR quarter's plan). IMPORTANT: quarter+offset must stay INSIDE the fiscal year - do not reference quarters before Q1; for a "coverage was X at the start of the year" claim use quarter=FY26-Q1 WITHOUT an offset and anchor the plan numbers accordingly.
- pipeline_concentration: {top_n_accounts, min_top_share_pct} - top N accounts' share of open-pipeline VALUE. Use for "coverage was concentrated in a few accounts/deals".
- effective_capacity: {target_pct, band_pp, unit?} - derived effective capacity pct ((actual-ramping)+ramping*ramp_pct/100)/plan must sit within band of target for every capacity-plan row (worst decides); optional unit narrows to one territory/region. Use for "we had the heads but not the capacity" ramp/hiring stories.
- quota_vs_potential: {dimension?, target_ratio_pct, band_pp, unit?} - a plan unit's total quota divided by its addressable market (summed account potential) as a percent. Use for "territory X was asked to sell more than exists" over-plan claims (target ~120) or whitespace headroom claims (target ~40).
- potential_coverage_gap: {dimension?, min_gap_pp} - top-half-potential units' share of CREATED pipeline lags their share of market potential by at least this many points. Use for "capacity went where the whitespace wasn't".
- headcount_growth_placement: {min_growth_share_pct} - at least this share of net headcount additions went to units that produced the bulk of first-quarter won revenue ("hires went to historically strong territories").
- unowned_account_share: {min_unowned_share_pct, top_value_pct?, quarter?} - among top-value accounts (default top 20% by won revenue), at least this share has NO owner in the evaluation quarter. Use for "key accounts were left unowned".
- post_change_revenue_decline: {min_gap_pp} - accounts whose owner changed show aggregate won-revenue growth trailing stable-owner accounts by at least this many points. Use for "revenue fell after accounts changed hands".
- forecast_vs_actual: {target_pct, band_pp} - the commit snapshot ran within band of this percent of actuals each quarter (109 = forecast was 9% hot). Use for "forecast said X but actuals came in Y".
- commit_no_engagement_share: {min_share_pct} - at least this share of COMMIT deal value sits on accounts with ZERO touches that quarter. Use for "half the commit wasn't being worked".
- core_vs_headline_growth: {min_headline_growth_pct, max_core_growth_pct} - total won-revenue growth first->last quarter reaches min_headline while ex-outlier (core) growth stays at or below max_core (typically negative). Use for "the beat was carried by a few huge deals while the core business shrank".
- elasticity_differential: {min_gap_pp} - won-rate change of LOW-potential accounts trails HIGH-potential accounts by at least this many points after price changes. Use for "the price increase hurt where we could least afford it".
- activity_potential_misalignment: {min_gap_pp} - low-potential accounts (bottom half by potential) receive more than their fair share (account-count share) of all touches by at least this many points. Use for "activity is up but aimed at the wrong accounts".

IMPORTANT semantics:
- realized_vs_list measures REVENUE RETAINED as a percentage of list price. Its targets are approximately 100 minus the discount percentages (e.g., discounts rising 12% to 18% means realized/list falls ~88% to ~82%), NOT the discount values themselves.
- Only reference regions/segments that plausibly exist in the data model (e.g., AMER, EMEA, APAC). Do not invent extra regions or tier names - when the story implies tiers without naming them, use entry/core/premium and say so in ambiguities.
- Margin claims are percentage-point changes (3 points of margin = target_change_pct=-3), NOT percent changes.
