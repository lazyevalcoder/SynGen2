Revenue missed plan by 4% this year, while sales capacity was technically sufficient on paper. AE headcount ended the year at 98% of plan, but ramping AEs made up a much larger share of the sales org than the annual plan had assumed, and a ramping rep produces only about half of what a tenured one does. Effective productive capacity was therefore closer to 87%, not the fully-staffed picture the plan painted. The miss was driven by a capacity planning assumption that treated all headcount as equally productive.
