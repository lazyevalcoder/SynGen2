Quota attainment declined 6 points this year even though rep capacity increased by 10%. The incremental headcount was added almost entirely to the territories with historically strong productivity, but those territories were already highly penetrated, so new AEs inherited limited whitespace and produced well below steady state during the year. Because the new hires were ramping, the capacity investment did not translate into proportional revenue: effective productive capacity stayed below the headline headcount growth, and attainment slipped to about 94% of plan.
