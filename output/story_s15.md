Revenue missed plan by about 5% this year despite Enterprise pipeline coverage remaining above 3x. The largest sales territories were carrying quotas set 20 to 25 percent above their estimated addressable market, making their numbers structurally unreachable, while several smaller territories held significant untapped whitespace but had been assigned comparatively modest quotas. This created a structural mismatch between quota setting, territory potential, and pipeline capacity rather than a simple execution problem.
