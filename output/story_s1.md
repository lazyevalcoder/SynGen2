NA revenue missed plan by 4% this year, driven primarily by Enterprise. Pipeline coverage looked healthy at around 3.4x, but it was concentrated in lower-potential territories and skewed toward early-stage deals, and expected close dates kept slipping into later quarters as the year went on. At the same time the Enterprise AEs were carrying well over plan-relative workload: ramping hires left effective productive capacity well below the headline headcount, so the reps actually covering Enterprise were effectively carrying about 118% of a fully-ramped capacity-adjusted quota. The gap points to a combined territory design, capacity planning, and pipeline quality problem rather than insufficient headline coverage.
